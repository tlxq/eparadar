# epaRadar roadmap

What works today, what comes next. Details of finished work live in the git history.

## Works today

- **Account**: sign-up with email confirmation, age 15+ checked on the server, account deletion in
  the app and on the web, data export
- **Friends**: friend codes, QR, nearby radar, suggestions, block and report everywhere
- **Map**: opt-in, friends-only live location with background tracking and ghost mode; spots,
  meetups, warnings and statuses that expire on the server
- **Feed**: friends' posts with photos, reactions and sharing to chat
- **Chat**: 1:1 and group chat with photos, reactions, read receipts, typing and unsend
- **Push notifications** without message text, admin view for reports
- **Website** eparadar.se with privacy policy, terms and community rules
